About Dhan Enterprise

A decade of renewable energy innovation — consulting, R&D, trade, and education from the heart of Hyderabad.

Our Story

A Decade of Clean Energy

Founded in 2014, Dhan Enterprise has grown from a boutique EV consulting practice into one of South India's most connected renewable energy firms.

We operate across EV systems, Solar PV, Battery Energy Storage, Wind, Green Hydrogen, and international trade — serving private companies, government bodies, and startups. Our Begumpet headquarters serves as the nerve centre for a 22-state network of 120+ partner organisations.
