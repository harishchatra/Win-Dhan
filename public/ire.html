Event Overview

About IRE Expo 2026

IRE 2026 is a premier Renewable Energy & Electric Mobility exhibition co-organized by Dhan Enterprise and Suprabha Trust. Designed as a key convergence node, the expo brings together state ministries, nodal bodies, central PSUs, global technology manufacturers, dealers, and corporate investors to network, trade, and exchange insights.

Spanning over two action-packed days in Hyderabad, the event will feature tender announcements, expert policy summits, commercial showcases, and live product launches defining the path of India's green grid transition.
