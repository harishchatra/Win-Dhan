About the Expo

What is IRE 2026?

IRE 2026 is Telangana's premier renewable energy and electric vehicle exposition — bringing together manufacturers, distributors, government bodies, investors, researchers, and innovators under one roof for two transformative days.

Co-organised by Dhan Enterprise and Suprabha Trust, IRE 2026 creates an unparalleled platform for B2B networking, live product demonstrations, policy discussions, and deal-making in India's booming clean energy sector.
