Who We Are

Powering India's
Clean Energy Future

Dhan Enterprise is a Hyderabad-based renewable energy consultancy founded in 2014 by Harish Manjunath. With over a decade of deep industry expertise at the intersection of automotive technology and sustainable energy, we bridge the gap between innovation and scalable implementation.

We work with OEMs, project developers, EPCs, government bodies, and international partners — providing end-to-end advisory across the entire renewable energy value chain.

We co-organise the Indian Renewable Energy Expo (IRE 2026) with Suprabha Trust — Telangana's premier platform connecting innovators, policymakers, investors, and energy leaders across India.
